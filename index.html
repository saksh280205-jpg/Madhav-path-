🌅 Daily Shloka of the Day

Chapter 2 · Verse 47
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन। मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥
You have a right to perform your prescribed duty, but never to the fruits of action. Never consider yourself the cause of the results, nor be attached to inaction.
